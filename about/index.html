About

A simpler way to prepare for the German citizenship exam

Leben in Deutschland is a free learning project designed to make exam preparation more approachable for people studying the German citizenship test and Life in Germany material.

The idea is simple: give learners a clear, accessible, and distraction-free study experience without forcing them through ads, subscriptions, or paywalls.

  • Free study support
  • No ads or interruptions
  • No subscription requirement
  • Quick access through App Clip and web experience
  • Focused content for citizenship exam preparation

This project exists to reduce friction for learners who want a calm, direct way to study and build confidence before the exam.
