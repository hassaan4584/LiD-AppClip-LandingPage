About Leben in Deutschland

Leben in Deutschland is a free learning project designed to support people preparing for the German citizenship exam and the Leben in Deutschland test.

The goal is simple: provide helpful preparation content in a way that is free to use, easy to access, and free from advertising interruptions.

  • Free study support
  • No ads
  • No subscription requirement
  • Practice for the German citizenship exam
  • Fast access via App Clip and web experience

This project exists to make learning more accessible and less stressful for people preparing for important official study material.
