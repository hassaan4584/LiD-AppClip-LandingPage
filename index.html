About

A clearer way to prepare

Leben in Deutschland is a free learning project created for people preparing for the German citizenship exam and Life in Germany test.

The goal is simple: make revision more approachable, easier to understand, and free from unnecessary distractions.

  • Free study support
  • No ads or interruptions
  • No subscription requirement
  • Quick access through App Clip and web

FAQ

Questions people usually ask

Is it free?

Yes. The project is designed to be free to use and accessible without a paywall.

Does it have ads?

No. The experience is meant to stay clean, focused, and distraction-free.

What does it help with?

It supports preparation for the German citizenship exam and the Leben in Deutschland test.

Is it available on iPhone?

Yes. It includes App Clip support for compatible iPhone devices and a direct web flow.

Is this official study material?

It is a study aid designed to help learners practice and prepare with confidence, not an official government publication.
