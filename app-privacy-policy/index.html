App Privacy

Privacy policy for the app

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Leben in Deutschland iOS app and App Clip handle information. It applies to the app experience only. The privacy practices of our website are described separately.

1. Data controller

The app is developed and operated by Hassaan Fayyaz Ahmed. Questions or requests about privacy can be sent to hassaanfayyaz@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app is designed to store learning data locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Saved or favourite questions
  • Question answers and attempt history
  • Practice-test results and practice-test history
  • Your primary and secondary app languages
  • Your selected German state
  • Speech, listening, shuffle, and display preferences
  • Daily reminder settings and the selected reminder time
  • Cached question images and downloaded official questionnaire PDFs
  • Local status used for app review or support prompts

This information is stored using Apple-provided device storage, such as UserDefaults and application storage. It is not uploaded to an app account or server by the app.

3. Data collection and analytics

The app does not require an account and does not request a name, email address, payment details, government identifier, or precise location.

The app does not currently include Firebase, advertising SDKs, third-party advertising trackers, or a third-party analytics SDK. It does not use third-party analytics to build a personal profile.

4. Network downloads and caches

The app may connect to the internet to download question images or an official questionnaire PDF and to display or cache that content. The image or file host may receive ordinary technical information from an internet request, such as an IP address, request time, device network information, or a record that a file was requested.

Cached images and downloaded PDFs remain in application storage until they are cleared by the app, removed through system storage controls, or deleted with the app.

5. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app requests permission to schedule local notifications on your device. Your reminder time and enabled or disabled status are stored locally.

Reminder notifications are scheduled on the device. The app does not send notification preferences to a server. You can turn reminders off in the app or change notification permission in iOS or macOS system settings.

6. User controls and deletion

You can control your data in several ways:

  • Reset question progress and attempt history in Settings
  • Clear saved or favourite questions in Settings
  • Clear practice-test history from the history screen
  • Disable daily reminders or revoke notification permission
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored app data and caches

7. External websites and services

Some app features open content or services outside the app. These actions occur only when you choose them. They include:

  • Apple App Store pages for app sharing or review links
  • Apple StoreKit review prompts
  • Support or donation pages and their payment providers
  • Your email application or Gmail/Outlook to compose an email
  • Third-party appointment providers, websites, or test information
  • Share sheets used to share the app or downloaded PDF

External providers process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not control or receive the payment information you enter on a payment provider’s website. The app does not receive that payment information.

8. Clipboard and sharing

The appointment email feature can copy an email template to the system clipboard. The app may also use Apple share sheets to share the app or a PDF. Clipboard contents or shared data are handled by iOS and the app, service, or recipient you select.

9. App Clip

The App Clip provides limited access to study questions and app features. It uses bundled question content and does not create an app account. Apple may process app distribution and App Clip launch information under Apple’s privacy terms. The App Clip does not add third-party analytics.

10. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data or share local study progress for advertising. Information is shared only when you use an external feature, share content, send an email, or otherwise choose to provide or transmit it to another service or person.

11. Retention

Local learning data and settings remain on the device until you clear them, delete the app, or remove them through system storage controls. Downloaded PDFs and cached images remain until removed or superseded. We do not maintain an app account or an app-server copy of this local data.

If you contact us by email or through a feedback form, we retain the correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain support records, and meet legal obligations.

12. Security

Device data is protected by Apple’s app and device security mechanisms and by the access controls you configure on your device. No method of storing or transmitting information is completely secure, and data can always be lost if a device is damaged, modified, or deleted.

13. GDPR and other privacy rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights to request access to personal data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, data portability, or withdrawal of consent. You can exercise these rights by emailing hassaanfayyaz@gmail.com.

Depending on the situation, the legal bases may include your consent, performance or provision of the app and requested features, and our legitimate interests in operating and maintaining the service. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in the country where you live or work or in the country where a suspected violation occurred.

14. Children’s privacy

The app is a study aid and does not knowingly create accounts or collect personal information from children. It is not designed to provide personalised advertising or marketing profiles. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information directly to us should contact us so that we can assess and address the request.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or privacy practices change. The latest effective date will appear at the top of this page. Material changes may also be announced through the website or app when appropriate.

16. Contact

For questions, deletion requests, or privacy concerns, contact: hassaanfayyaz@gmail.com
