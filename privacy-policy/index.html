Website privacy policy

This project is committed to keeping the learning experience simple, respectful, and transparent.

This policy describes the Leben in Deutschland website. It does not replace the privacy notice for the iOS or macOS app.

Read the Leben in Deutschland app privacy policy for information about on-device study progress, local notifications, downloads, App Clip behaviour, sharing, and external app features.

The website is a static informational and study-support experience. It does not currently require an account, collect quiz answers for an account, or use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Our hosting provider or content delivery platform may receive standard technical request information, such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and request time, for hosting and security purposes. We do not sell personal data or use it to build marketing profiles.

If you contact the project or support channels, information you provide may be used to respond to your enquiry or provide support. We do not use that information for unrelated marketing without clear consent.

The website may contain links to external providers, including app stores, support and payment pages, email services, and appointment providers. Those providers handle their own data under their own privacy policies.

This policy may be updated over time. The latest effective date is November 25, 2026.
