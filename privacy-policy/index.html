Privacy policy

This project is committed to keeping the learning experience simple, respectful, and transparent.

The website is primarily a static informational and study-support experience. It may use basic analytics, hosting logs, or technical diagnostics for maintenance and performance monitoring, depending on the platform or hosting provider used.

We do not sell personal data. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information for the purpose of creating user profiles or marketing lists.

If third-party services are used for analytics, app distribution, or hosting, their privacy policies may apply. Please review the relevant provider documentation where applicable.

If you contact the project or support channels, any information you provide may be used to respond to your inquiry or provide support. We do not use that information for unrelated marketing purposes without clear consent.

This project may be updated over time. We recommend checking this page periodically for changes.
