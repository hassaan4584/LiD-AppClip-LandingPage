Study guide

A simple study guide for the Life in Germany exam

A good preparation strategy is not about studying everything at once. It is about learning the main categories, practicing consistently, and reviewing difficult questions until they feel familiar.

Recommended study plan

  1. Review one major topic at a time
  2. Answer a short set of practice questions
  3. Mark repeat mistakes and revisit them later
  4. Take a mock test once you feel more confident
  5. Repeat weak areas before the real exam

Core areas to cover

  • Democracy and the rule of law
  • Rights and responsibilities
  • History and national identity
  • Education, work, and society
  • Daily life and civic values

Keep your sessions short and consistent. Regular revision tends to work better than intense study over a short period of time.
