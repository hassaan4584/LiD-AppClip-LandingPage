Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes. The project is designed to be free to use and accessible without a paywall.

Does it contain ads?

No. The design goal is an ad-free, distraction-free learning experience.

What is it for?

It supports preparation for the German citizenship exam and the Leben in Deutschland test.

Is it available on iPhone?

Yes. It includes App Clip support for compatible iPhone devices and a direct web entry flow.
